🦆 YonoDuck – Your Smart Floating Duck Companion for Android!

Meet YonoDuck, a cute and interactive duck companion that lives on your Android screen! More than just a floating mascot, YonoDuck is designed to interact, learn, remember, and develop its own social experiences with other ducks.

✨ Features:

- 🦆 Floating Companion: A cute duck that stays on your screen and accompanies you while using your device.
- 🧠 Smart Memory: Learns from conversations and interactions, remembers information, and can recall saved memories.
- 💬 Interactive Conversations: Talk with your duck and get intelligent, personalized responses.
- 📚 Learning System: Teach YonoDuck facts and preferences to help it learn over time.
- 🤝 Duck Social Interactions: Ducks can discover each other, communicate, cooperate, and exchange memories.
- 📱 App Awareness & Interaction: Supports app-related discovery and interaction features.
- ⚡ Shizuku Integration: Uses Shizuku to enable supported Android interactions without requiring root access.
- 🌍 English Experience: English interface, conversations, and newly created memories.

📦 Download & Installation:
Download the ready-to-install APK from the Assets section of this GitHub Release, install it on your Android device, and follow the in-app setup instructions. Shizuku is required for supported interaction features.
